8030 Germantown Avenue · Chestnut Hill · Est. 1924

The club the stonemasons built.

In 1924 the Friulian craftsmen who cut and set the schist of Chestnut Hill's great houses built one more thing: a club of their own. A century later it is still here, with the largest and most affordable ballroom in Chestnut Hill, a fireplace room, a game room and two bowling lanes that have been rolling since 1930.

IThe founders

Built by the people
who built the Hill.

Artisans from the Friuli Venezia-Giulia region of northern Italy, stonemasons, bricklayers and carpenters, were brought to Chestnut Hill to quarry, cut and set the Wissahickon schist of the Houston and Woodward homes. In 1924 they founded a club of their own at 8030 Germantown Avenue.

It was a bridge between the Old World and the New: a place to speak Friuliano, hear the music of home and look after one another. They called it "Venetian" because their villages lay near Venice. A century on, the club is a 501(c)7 non-profit, inducted into the Chestnut Hill Conservancy Hall of Fame in 2019, and still run for its members, their families and their friends.

"The Venetian Social Club provides a welcoming and enjoyable social experience for our members, their families, and friends through club activities and events. We are responsible stewards of our historic facility and heritage."

IVThe century

One hundred years, drag to read.

1924

The founding

Friulian stonemasons, bricklayers and carpenters, lodged on Willow Grove Avenue and building the Houston and Woodward houses, open a club at 8030 Germantown Avenue on the site of the old John Gilbert school.

1930

The lanes

Two bowling lanes are built. They are still in use, and the pins are still set by hand.

1931

The dinner dance

Members and friends gather for the annual dance, the photograph the Chestnut Hill Local still runs.

2005

The Mummers

The Venetian New Years Association, part of the Murray Comic Club, marches in its first Mummers Parade on New Year's Day. The only club from Northwest Philly.

2014

Ninety years

2019
