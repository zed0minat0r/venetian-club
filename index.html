A hundred years on Germantown Avenue.

Founded in 1924 by the stonemasons and carpenters who built Chestnut Hill, the Venetian Social Club is still the neighborhood's living room: a private members' club, the largest and most affordable ballroom in Chestnut Hill, and two bowling lanes that have been rolling since 1930.

Our club

Built by the people who built the Hill.

In 1924, artisans from the Friuli Venezia-Giulia region of northern Italy, brought to Chestnut Hill to quarry, cut and set the Wissahickon schist of the Houston and Woodward homes, founded a club of their own at 8030 Germantown Avenue.

It was a bridge between the Old World and the New: a place to speak Friuliano, hear the music of home and look after one another. A century later the Venetian Social Club is a 501(c)7 non-profit, inducted into the Chestnut Hill Conservancy Hall of Fame in 2019, and still run for its members, their families and their friends.

"The Venetian Social Club provides a welcoming and enjoyable social experience for our members, their families, and friends through club activities and events. We are responsible stewards of our historic facility and heritage."

Traditions

Some things we have never stopped doing.

1930

The bowling league

Two original working lanes and ten regular teams who bowl up to six nights a week during the season. The pins are still set by hand.

2005

The Chestnut Hill Mummers

The Venetian New Years Association is part of the Murray Comic Club and marches every New Year's Day in the Philadelphia Mummers Parade, Comic Brigade Division. The only club representing Northwest Philly.

Friûl

Friulian heritage

From frico and San Nicolò to the Poffabro nativities and the Epiphany bonfires, the club keeps the customs of the founders' villages alive with talks, dinners and the centennial series.

100

The centennial

A year of monthly events led up to the 100th anniversary banquet in September 2024, from storytelling afternoons with the founding families to Christmas in Poffabro.
