Heritage

Built by the people who built the Hill.

In 1924, stonemasons, bricklayers and carpenters from the Friuli Venezia-Giulia region of northern Italy, brought to Chestnut Hill to quarry, cut and set the Wissahickon schist of the Houston and Woodward homes, founded a club of their own at 8030 Germantown Avenue. They called it Venetian because their villages lay near Venice.

A century on, the Venetian Social Club is a 501(c)7 non-profit, inducted into the Chestnut Hill Conservancy Hall of Fame in 2019, and still run for its members, their families and their friends.

Our mission

The Venetian Social Club provides a welcoming and enjoyable social experience for our members, their families, and friends through club activities and events. We continue our role as a valuable and trusted partner supporting culture, charity causes and groups within our members' communities. We are responsible stewards of our historic facility and heritage.

Membership

Three hundred members. Two sponsors.

The Venetian Club is a private social club for the enjoyment and betterment of its membership. Membership is limited to 300 individuals; dues are paid annually.

Traditions

Some things we have never stopped doing.

Since 1930

The bowling league

Two original working lanes and ten regular teams who bowl up to six nights a week during the season. Pinsetters still set the pins by hand.

Since 2005

The Chestnut Hill Mummers

The Venetian New Years Association marches every New Year's Day in the Philadelphia Mummers Parade with the Murray Comic Club. The only club representing Northwest Philly.

Since 1924

Friuli

The founders' region, kept alive with dinners, talks and the centennial series, from a storytelling afternoon with the founding families to Christmas in Poffabro.
